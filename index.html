About the Coach

Forty-plus years on the same corner of Florin Road.

Always Fresh

We don't compromise on quality. Grits, gravy, and biscuits are made from scratch every morning, with no shortcuts.

Never Frozen

Our chicken fried steak is hand-breaded and cooked to order. If it's on the plate, it wasn't sitting in a freezer first.
