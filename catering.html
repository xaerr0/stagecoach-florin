Stagecoach After Hours

Book the dining room after close.

We open our doors from 5pm to 10pm, every evening except Sunday and recognized holidays, for full plated-dinner service with our own staff. A minimum of 20 to 50 guests is required per event.

How It Works

What's included.

Time & Setup

  • First 2 hours included with meal price
  • Each additional hour until 10pm is $100/hr
  • Designated outside area for smoking
  • Lectern, background music, and portable P.A. with microphone provided

Service

  • All meals served tableside by our staff
  • Staff sets up, serves, and clears tables
  • Tablecloths provided
  • 20–50 guest minimum per event

Booking & Payment

  • $40 per person, plus tax
  • Completed meal order due 3 days before the event
  • Partial payment due at time of booking
  • Full payment due 3 days before the event, by credit card or cash only
